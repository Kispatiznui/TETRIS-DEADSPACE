THE SYSTEM

The rules are familiar. The consequences are not.

TETRIS: DEADSPACE takes the fundamental language of falling blocks and turns it into a survival system.

Pieces fall. Lines are built. Space becomes limited. The player continues to construct because construction is the only process the system still knows how to perform.

01

BUILD

Place, rotate and manipulate pieces to keep the structure alive.

02

CLEAR

Complete lines to create space, score and influence the state of the system.

03

SURVIVE

Manage more than the board. Your resources and the reality around you are also deteriorating.

08 SOMETHING IS OBSERVING

THE
DEEP MAW

THE DEEP MAW is not an enemy you can defeat.

There is no health bar. There is no conventional boss fight. There is no strategy that allows the player to kill it.

Its presence grows as the systems around the player deteriorate. The deeper the instability becomes, the more influence the presence can exert over the experience.

FEEDBACK // CONTEXTUAL
09

FEEDBACK

The game does not always tell you what is happening.

TETRIS: DEADSPACE uses contextual feedback instead of filling the screen with permanent explanations.

MSG MESSAGES

System communication can provide warnings, clues or uncertainty.

SIG SIGNALS

Interruptions and anomalies can communicate changes indirectly.

VIS VISUAL

The board and environment respond to changing system conditions.

AUD AUDIO

Sound can reveal states that the interface refuses to explain.

NOT EVERYTHING IS EXPLAINED. SOME THINGS MUST BE OBSERVED.
